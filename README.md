# Jam


Jam is a free browser app for beginners in music. It lets people who don't own an instrument play piano, bass, and drums together with their friends.

Play it here: https://jam-7j9l.onrender.com

No download or account needed. It works on a computer, tablet, or phone.

Features

- Three instruments: piano, bass, and drums
- Rooms: create a room, share the 4-letter code, and friends join from their own devices
- Free Jam: play anything you want, alone or with friends
- Song Mode: a rhythm game where you follow falling notes, solo or as a band
- Available in English and Korean

Why I made it

I have played piano since I was six, and the most fun part of music for me has always been playing with other people. I made Jam so that beginners can have that experience too, even without an instrument or lessons.
